# OSDS — Scientific Reference Register

**Observational Systems and Data Sources** · Work in progress

A scientific discovery register of observation networks, monitoring infrastructure, archives and data platforms. All **62 entries are candidates**: coverage and observation-category assignments remain unverified, and no complete source record or dataset has been independently verified. The authoritative [machine reference](OSDS_Machine_Reference.md) provides detailed classifications, evidence and unresolved research questions.

## Reading key

Source identifiers are SRC-001–SRC-062, qualified as `OSDS:SRC-001` across projects. D01–D08 and O01–O08 are OSDS-specific classifications, not approved DRM-wide codes or equivalents of EII's domain codes. Sources may belong to several domains and categories; the tables group them by their primary presentation domain. Observation descriptions express the recorded category assignments, not independently verified measurement capabilities. Each OSDS ID links to its machine record, and each source name links to its official discovery page.

## Discovery links and access limitations — 22 October 2026

Links identify official sources or discovery pages; they do not establish dataset availability or quality. Some checks used indexed official pages where direct access was blocked. OSCAR/Surface displayed a test-environment notice and its operational link redirected to the same page; ISC displayed a temporary outage notice. Lincoln is a collection of agricultural datasets, AG-LOAM resolves to its maintainer's dataset documentation, and the historical NREL label now links to a current NLR grid data/tools page. Details and evidence are retained per record in the machine register.

## Scientific interpretation

These are discovery routes, not interchangeable measurement datasets. Station and site registries may expose metadata; repositories may hold heterogeneous research outputs; coordinated services may combine observations, estimates and models. Category membership does not establish that a source supplies calibrated measurements or open data. Select a specific product and inspect its method, units, quality, time span and rights before analytical use.

Preserved entries with possible overlap include ICOS / ICOS Ecosystem Stations (005/040), EarthScope / EarthScope Research Infrastructure (022/062), Zenodo / Zenodo Research Datasets (055/058), and NASA Open Data / NASA Earthdata (053/057). These remain separate pending identity and scope review. Agricultural placement of EFAS and the scope of broad repository entries also require review.

## Foundational standards and interoperability

[SOSA / SSN — Semantic Sensor Network Ontology](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-ssn-2017/), [OGC SensorThings API](https://www.ogc.org/standards/sensorthings/), and [OGC Observations, Measurements and Samples (OMS)](https://www.ogc.org/standards/om/) are reference foundations for later entity and access modelling. Editions, formal mappings and implementation claims remain unverified.
